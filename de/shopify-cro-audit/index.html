WHAT WE TYPICALLY FIX

Patterns we see again and again on Shopify fashion and beauty stores — and the layouts we recommend instead.

Finding

Hero with no next step

One photo and a single Shop Now. We add clear entry points — Shop Men / Shop Women — right in the hero.

Finding

Trust buried below

Authenticity, shipping and returns only in the footer. We move them into a trust bar under the hero.

Finding

Crowded filters

All filters in one messy row. We add quick category chips and a cleaner sort / filter bar.

Details hidden in tabs

Size guide, shipping and returns collapsed far from the button. We group them with price and Add to Bag.

Generic cart drawer

A plain Buy button. We add order summary, Complete the Look, and Secure Checkout with trust copy.
